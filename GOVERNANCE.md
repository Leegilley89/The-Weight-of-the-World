# Governance: The Weight of the World

**Model: Founder-led, community-developed.**

The founder directs the game's core identity and approves major creative, licensing, funding, and release decisions. Community contributors may propose features, submit code and content, review changes, and help manage workstreams.

## Roles
- **Founder / project lead:** Final approval of the design pillars, roadmap, maintainers, branding, and official releases.
- **Maintainers:** Appointed by the founder to review changes, prioritize issues, and enforce quality and safety standards.
- **Contributors:** Anyone may suggest improvements, fork the repository, and submit pull requests.
- **Community:** Players and supporters can test builds, report problems, and participate in design discussions.

## Decisions
1. Discuss large changes in an issue before implementing them.
2. Submit isolated, documented pull requests.
3. Maintainers review code quality, licensing, accessibility, performance, and alignment with the game's pillars.
4. The founder resolves disputed major decisions and authorizes official releases.
5. Keep project decisions publicly documented whenever practical.

## Protected design pillars
- Open-world survival and crafting.
- Moral choices alter gameplay, abilities, NPCs, factions, and story.
- First-person default with a switchable third-person camera.
- Broad object interaction, persistent containers, ownership, and theft.
- A welcoming, transparent volunteer development process.

## Contributions and ownership
Submission does not guarantee acceptance, payment, employment, or a share of crowdfunding proceeds. Code contributions are submitted under the repository's MIT license; art, music, writing, and other assets must have explicit licensing and provenance before inclusion. Contributors retain rights as provided by the applicable licenses.

## Crowdfunding
Any Kickstarter campaign requires a separately published scope, budget, risk statement, estimated delivery approach, and fulfillment responsibilities. Fundraising decisions remain with the founder; backers do not automatically gain governance rights.
