# Funding and Kickstarter Preparation

Kickstarter is not live. This document is a planning guide, not a campaign promise.

## First principle
Release a demonstrable vertical slice before soliciting crowdfunding. Publish gameplay footage, current limitations, known risks, and a reproducible build.

## Funding uses
Possible budget categories: contractor art/audio, accessibility, performance optimization, testing, production tooling, legal/accounting, platform fees, and contingency.

## Required before launch
- Defined scope and production milestones
- Transparent budget and funding target
- Rights and licenses for all showcased content
- Clear distinction between volunteer contributions and paid contracts
- Reward tiers and realistic fulfillment planning
- Tax and business structure reviewed professionally
- Accountable owner responsible for funds and backers
- Public risk disclosures and regular progress updates

## Principle
Open-source contribution does not entitle a contributor to revenue or equity unless a separate written agreement exists. Nor does Kickstarter funding provide backers an ownership stake in code or assets.
