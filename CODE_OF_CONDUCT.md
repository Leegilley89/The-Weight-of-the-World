# Code of Conduct

We welcome developers, artists, designers, writers, testers, and players regardless of experience level or background. Be respectful, constructive, and professional.

Do not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate, share personal information without consent, or intentionally disrupt collaboration. Assume good faith, discuss ideas based on evidence, and respect reasonable boundaries.

Report issues privately to repository maintainers using GitHub's available private contact channels. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers may moderate discussions, close issues, remove material, or restrict participation where needed. The founder has final responsibility for enforcement.
